Student Practice
Learning focus: Solve and represent two-step problems, then check whether answers are reasonable.
Directions: Complete all items. Show calculations, cite evidence, or explain your reasoning when requested. Check
your work before submitting.
1. A class packs 6 boxes with 8 markers each, then gives away 15 markers. How many remain?
2. Mia reads 24 pages Monday and 18 Tuesday. Her book has 70 pages. How many remain?
3. Four teams collect 9 cans each and then collect 17 more. Total?
4. A garden has 63 plants in 7 equal rows. Three plants in each row bloom. How many bloom?
5. A store had 85 notebooks, sold 29, then received 40. How many now?
6. Five buses carry 7 students each. Twelve more arrive by car. Total?
7. Write an equation for 9 bags of 4 apples with 11 apples eaten.
8. A player earns 8 points in each of 3 rounds and loses 5 penalty points. Score?

Exit Ticket
Complete independently in 8-10 minutes.
1. State the main skill or concept in your own words.
2. Create one accurate example that uses today’s skill.
3. Explain how you checked your example.
4. Identify one common error and correct it.
5. Rate your confidence from 1-4 and name your next practice step.
Quick rubric: 4 = accurate, complete, and well justified; 3 = accurate with minor omission; 2 = partial
understanding; 1 = limited evidence; 0 = blank or unrelated.
