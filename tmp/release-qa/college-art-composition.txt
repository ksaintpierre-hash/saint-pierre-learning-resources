Level: College - Art Appreciation

Subject: Fine Arts
Resource type: Analysis Packet
Estimated time: 45-60 minutes
Standard / objective: Course objective - Analyze how line, shape, color, value, texture, space, emphasis,
balance, rhythm, and unity affect meaning in original compositions.
Source note: Course-level learning objective; no accreditation, transfer, or universal equivalency claimed. All included geometric studies
are original.
What is included: branded cover, teacher guide, student directions, 16 original practice items, 5-item exit ticket,
complete answer key/worked guidance, differentiation supports, printing notes, and license statement.

Preparation: Print student pages single-sided or assign through a secure LMS. Review the key and select tools

Exit Ticket
Complete independently in 8-10 minutes.
1. State the main skill or concept in your own words.
2. Create one accurate example that uses today’s skill.
3. Explain how you checked your example.
4. Identify one common error and correct it.
5. Rate your confidence from 1-4 and name your next practice step.
Quick rubric: 4 = accurate, complete, and well justified; 3 = accurate with minor omission; 2 = partial
understanding; 1 = limited evidence; 0 = blank or unrelated.
