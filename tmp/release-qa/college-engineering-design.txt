Student Practice
Learning focus: Design a low-cost paper structure that supports a specified load while documenting decisions
and test evidence.
Directions: Complete all items. Show calculations, cite evidence, or explain your reasoning when requested. Check
your work before submitting.

2. Define constraint.
3. Given max 10 sheets, identify the constraint.
4. Given support 500 g, identify the criterion.
5. Why generate multiple concepts?
6. Name a fair-test variable to control.
7. What is a prototype?
8. Why record failure location?

Exit Ticket
Complete independently in 8-10 minutes.
1. State the main skill or concept in your own words.
2. Create one accurate example that uses today’s skill.
3. Explain how you checked your example.
4. Identify one common error and correct it.
5. Rate your confidence from 1-4 and name your next practice step.
Quick rubric: 4 = accurate, complete, and well justified; 3 = accurate with minor omission; 2 = partial
understanding; 1 = limited evidence; 0 = blank or unrelated.

Complete Teacher Answer Key
1. A desired feature or measure of success.

3. Use no more than 10 sheets.

14. Answers vary; must identify design change and measurable outcome.
