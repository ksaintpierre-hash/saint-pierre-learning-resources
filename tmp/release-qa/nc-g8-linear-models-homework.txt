Teacher Guide
Level: Grade 8
State: North Carolina
Subject: Mathematics
Resource type: Homework
Estimated time: 40-50 minutes
Standard / objective: NC.8.F.4 - Analyze functions that model linear relationships; determine and interpret rate of
change and initial value from two values or from a graph, table, or description.
Source note: North Carolina Department of Public Instruction, Mathematics Standards Vertical Progression:
https://www.dpi.nc.gov/documents/publications/catalog/ma196-vertical-progression-math/open; NC Check-Ins 2.0 Mathematics
Specifications: https://www.dpi.nc.gov/documents/accountability/testing/nc-checkin/nc-check-ins-20-mathematics-grades-3-8-specifications
(accessed September 14, 2026).
What is included: branded cover, teacher guide, student directions, 16 original practice items, 5-item exit ticket,
complete answer key/worked guidance, differentiation supports, printing notes, and license statement.
Purpose: Build linear models from tables and contexts and interpret slope and initial value with units.
Preparation: Print student pages single-sided or assign through a secure LMS. Review the key and select tools
allowed for your learners. No special commercial curriculum is required.
Access and differentiation
AIG: require a second representation, proof, counterexample, transfer task, or student-created extension. 504:
provide extended time, chunked sections, breaks, reduced copying, and accessible digital zoom as specified by
the plan. EC: preteach vocabulary, model one item, provide manipulatives/visuals, and accept aligned response
modes from the IEP. ESL: preview key terms, provide bilingual resources and sentence frames, and allow partner
rehearsal. Use each learner’s current plan; this guide does not replace it.

Student Practice
Learning focus: Build linear models from tables and contexts and interpret slope and initial value with units.
Directions: Complete all items. Show calculations, cite evidence, or explain your reasoning when requested. Check
your work before submitting.
1. Find slope through (2,7) and (6,19).
2. Write y=mx+b when m=4 and b=-2.
3. Table: x 0,2,4; y 5,11,17. Find m and b.
4. A taxi charges $4 plus $2.50 per mile. Model.
5. Interpret 4 in the taxi model.
6. Find taxi cost for 8 miles.
7. Line through (3,10) with slope 2. Find b.
8. Compare y=3x+1 and y=3x-5.

Exit Ticket
Complete independently in 8-10 minutes.
1. State the main skill or concept in your own words.
2. Create one accurate example that uses today’s skill.
3. Explain how you checked your example.
4. Identify one common error and correct it.
5. Rate your confidence from 1-4 and name your next practice step.
Quick rubric: 4 = accurate, complete, and well justified; 3 = accurate with minor omission; 2 = partial
understanding; 1 = limited evidence; 0 = blank or unrelated.
