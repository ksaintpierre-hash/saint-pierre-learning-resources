# Saint Pierre Learning Resources — approved collection expansion

Review date: September 15, 2026

90 new, complete products are prepared for the approved public catalog update. They supply 30 distinct, relevant bundles in each of the 19 previously empty resource-type collections. A bundle appears in multiple collections only when it contains the corresponding component; these placements are not 570 separately authored products. Purchasing is disabled for these 90 new resources. A newer store deployment already enabled Live PayPal for existing resources; its payment settings are preserved.

The catalog will contain 100 listings after deployment: 10 existing listings plus 90 new resource previews. New complete files are held behind owner authentication or a verified paid entitlement. Public pages expose only thumbnails, selected sample pages, and listing details.

## Coverage and limits

- Early grade filters include the 30 adult planning toolkits with explicit developmental adaptations. These are not 30 new Pre-K, Kindergarten, Grade 1, or Grade 2 academic worksheet packs.
- New state-standard alignment is North Carolina mathematics only. State-neutral educator and exam resources are not credited as standards coverage for all 50 states. The tracker retains all 50 states and identifies gaps for later batches.
- SAT and ACT collections contain shared mathematics skill practice, not complete exams, every tested subject, official released questions, or score predictions.
- Classroom décor consists of original typographic displays and functional classroom-layout diagrams; no classroom photographs are promised.
- Each academic bundle practices a focused part of a verified standard. It is not a full-grade curriculum or a complete EOG simulation.
- Previously uploaded novel-study files and Katia’s ELA slides were not altered or published by this expansion. The prior private inventory is preserved.
